Report an entry

This archive has no accounts and no moderation queue. If an entry breaks the rules below, report it and it will be reviewed and removed.

What gets removed

  • Links, email addresses, phone numbers, or other contact details
  • Sexually explicit content
  • Hate speech, slurs, or content that targets a person or group
  • Content that identifies a real person without their consent
  • Spam, scams, or anything illegal

How to report

Open an issue on the project page and include the entry code (for example AR-2026-XXXXX) and what is wrong with it. Do not post personal information about anyone in the issue.

Open a report

If you are the person who created the entry, you do not need to report it — use the manage link you saved when you created it to delete it yourself.
