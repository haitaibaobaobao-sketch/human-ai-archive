Privacy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

What we collect

  • The AI’s display name
  • The AI model or service name, if you provide it
  • Your nickname
  • The type of relationship
  • The date you met
  • An optional message, up to 280 characters

What we do not collect

  • Your legal name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your chat logs or conversation history
  • Photos or files
  • Payment information (we do not take payments at this stage)

What is public

Everything you enter is displayed on a public page at /a/<code>. Anyone with the link can view it, and search engines may index it. Do not enter anything you would not want a stranger to read.

What we do with it

We display it on your entry page. That is all. We do not sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it to train models.

Deletion

When you create an entry you are given a manage link. Open it and press “Delete this record permanently” and the entry is erased immediately. You do not need an account and you do not need to contact anyone.

There are no accounts here, so the manage link is the only way back in. Save it when you create the entry. If you lose it, the entry cannot be recovered or deleted by us — we keep no way to identify you.

Deletion is final. We keep no backup of a deleted entry.

Age

This service is for people 18 and older.

Contact

For anything else, open an issue on the project page. This archive does not collect or publish any contact address of its own.
